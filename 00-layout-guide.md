# Layout Guide: Haltom City Water Damage Site

This site uses a "Field Report" layout. It should not look like the Buckeye, Caldwell, Farmers Branch or Downers Grove builds.

## Global rules
- No forms anywhere. The only way to reach us is the phone number: (817) XXX-XXXX
- Sticky bottom call bar on mobile with one big "Tap to Call" button
- Top bar: logo on the left, phone number on the right, a simple text menu underneath (no mega menu)
- Color direction: deep navy with a warm amber accent (for calls to action) on off white paper backgrounds
- Typography: a bold slab serif for headings, clean sans serif for body text

## What makes it different
- Hero is a split panel: headline and phone on the left, a "Right Now" status card on the right (response time, crew status, open hours)
- Pages use a numbered "field notes" style where sections are labeled 01, 02, 03 down the left margin on desktop
- Service pages use a horizontal timeline strip for the process instead of cards or icon grids
- Neighborhood pages open with a "Local Snapshot" box (housing age, common water issues, nearby landmark) before any copy
- FAQs are shown as a two column Q and A layout, not an accordion
- Footer is a single wide call panel with the phone number in large type, then small link columns

## Section rhythm
Alternate full width text sections with narrow "callout" strips. Never place more than two card grids on one page.
