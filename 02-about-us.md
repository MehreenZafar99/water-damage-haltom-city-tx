## 01. Who We Are

Our technicians have worked water losses of every size, from a leaking dishwasher in a Browning Heights ranch house to a flooded office on Denton Highway. We know the housing here, the soil, the weather and the kinds of plumbing failures that show up again and again in Tarrant County.

**Tell the truth.** If drywall can be dried instead of torn out, we say so. If something has to go, we show you why with moisture readings.

**Finish the job.** We do not dry your home and disappear. We handle the repairs too.

**Respect your space.** We lay down floor protection, keep work areas tidy and treat your property like our own.

---

## 03. Training and Standards

Our crew follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration and S520 for mold. That means we use proven drying methods, check moisture levels every day and only stop when readings show the structure is truly dry.

Our equipment includes:
- Truck mounted and portable extractors
- Low grain refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers
- High velocity air movers
- Thermal imaging cameras and pin and pinless moisture meters
- HEPA air scrubbers for mold and sewage jobs

## 04. Why Local Matters

Haltom City has its own patterns. Older homes near Diamond Oaks with original plumbing. Slab leaks from shifting clay. Creek runoff near Fossil Creek during heavy rain. Knowing these patterns helps us find hidden water faster and plan the right drying setup from day one.
