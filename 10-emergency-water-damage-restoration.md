## 01. What Counts as a Water Emergency

Call us right away if you see any of these:
- Water actively spraying or pouring from a pipe, fixture or appliance
- Standing water on floors that is spreading to other rooms
- A ceiling that is bulging, dripping or sagging
- Water near electrical outlets, panels or appliances
- Sewage coming up through drains or toilets
- Storm water entering through the roof, windows or doors

If it is getting worse by the minute, it is an emergency.

---

## 02. The First Hour Matters Most

Here is what happens to a wet home over time:

| Time | What Happens |
|---|---|
| First few minutes | Water spreads across floors and soaks into carpet and pad |
| 1 to 24 hours | Drywall swells, furniture stains, wood starts absorbing water |
| 24 to 48 hours | Mold can begin to grow, odors start |
| 48 hours to 1 week | Wood floors cup, drywall crumbles, mold spreads |

Every hour you wait adds to the repair bill. Fast response keeps more of your home savable.
