## 01. Why Extraction Comes First

Fans alone cannot handle standing water. Removing liquid water with extractors is many times faster than waiting for it to evaporate. The more water we pull out at the start, the shorter your drying time and the less material has to be replaced.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a shop vac instead?**
A shop vac helps with small spills, but it cannot pull water out of carpet pad or subfloors. Leftover moisture leads to odors and mold.

**Will my carpet be ruined?**
Not always. If the water is clean and we get there quickly, carpet can often be dried and saved. Pad is sometimes replaced.

**How long does extraction take?**
Most rooms take one to three hours depending on how much water there is.

**Do you remove water from garages?**
Yes. We extract from garages, patios near entry points and any indoor space.
