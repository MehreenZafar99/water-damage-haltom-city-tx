## 01. Why Pipes Burst in Haltom City Homes

**Freezing.** North Texas homes are not always built for hard freezes. Pipes in attics, garages and exterior walls are most at risk.

**Old pipes.** Many homes here were built in the 1950s and 1960s with galvanized steel lines that rust from the inside out.

**High water pressure.** Pressure that is too high strains joints and fittings over time.

**Ground movement.** Clay soil shifting under slabs can crack pipes below and inside the foundation.

**Fitting failures.** Cheap supply lines under sinks and toilets split without warning.

## 02. Where to Find Your Shutoff

Most Haltom City homes have the main shutoff:
- Near the water meter at the front of the property, in a ground box
- In the garage near the water heater
- Inside a closet on an exterior wall

Knowing this before an emergency can save thousands in damage.

## 04. Signs of a Hidden Pipe Leak

Not every burst is dramatic. Watch for:
- A sudden jump in your water bill
- Warm or wet spots on the floor
- Bubbling paint or wallpaper
- Musty smells in one room
- The sound of running water when nothing is on

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does insurance cover burst pipes?**
Usually yes, because it is sudden and accidental. The cost of fixing the pipe itself may not be covered, but the water damage often is.

**Should I turn on the faucets after a freeze?**
Once the main valve is off, opening faucets drains remaining water and relieves pressure.

**How long does drying take after a burst pipe?**
Most jobs dry in three to five days.

**Can you fix the pipe?**
We handle the water damage. For the pipe repair, we can point you to a licensed plumber.
