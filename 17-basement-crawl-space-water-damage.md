## 01. Signs of Water Under Your Home

- Musty or damp smells inside the house
- Soft, bouncy or sagging floors
- Floors that feel cold and clammy
- Higher humidity indoors
- Visible water or mud when you look through the crawl space vent
- Insects or rodents around the foundation

---

## 02. How Water Gets Down There

- **Heavy rain** draining toward the foundation
- **Plumbing leaks** from supply and drain lines running under the house
- **Poor grading** that slopes water toward the home
- **Broken or missing gutters** dumping water at the foundation
- **High humidity** causing condensation on pipes and wood

---

## 03. What Water Does to a Crawl Space

Wet crawl spaces lead to wood rot in floor joists and beams, mold growth, sagging floors, pest problems and poor air quality inside your home. Air from the crawl space rises into living areas, so what happens below affects what you breathe.
