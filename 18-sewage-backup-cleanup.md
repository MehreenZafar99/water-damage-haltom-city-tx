## 01. Stay Safe Until We Arrive

- Keep children and pets out of the affected rooms
- Do not flush toilets or run water in the home
- Do not touch sewage without gloves and boots
- Turn off the HVAC system if it pulls air from the affected area
- Throw away any food that came into contact with the water

## 02. Why Sewage Backs Up in Haltom City

**Tree roots.** Mature trees are common here, and their roots crack and clog older clay and cast iron sewer lines.
**Aging pipes.** Sewer lines in homes from the 1950s and 1960s often have sagging, cracked or collapsed sections.
**Heavy rain.** Storms can overload the city system and push water back through floor drains.
**Clogs.** Grease, wipes and debris build up over time.

---

## 03. What Makes Sewage Cleanup Different

Sewage is rated Category 3, or black water. The cleanup follows stricter rules:
- Crews wear full protective gear
- Porous items like carpet, pad and soaked drywall are removed and bagged
- Every hard surface is cleaned and treated with disinfectant
- HEPA air scrubbers clean the air during work
- Waste is disposed of properly

No. Carpet and pad touched by sewage should be removed and replaced.

Yes. Proper cleaning, drying and deodorizing removes the odor completely.
