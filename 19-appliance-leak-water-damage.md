## 01. Appliances We Handle

**Water Heaters**
Tank failures, leaking valves and corroded bottoms. Many Haltom City homes keep water heaters in the garage, a closet or the attic, and attic failures are the worst because water comes through the ceiling.

**Washing Machines**
Burst supply hoses and overflowing drums. Rubber hoses should be replaced every five years.

**Dishwashers**
Leaks from worn door seals, loose connections and drain problems. Water often spreads under cabinets and flooring.

**Refrigerators and Ice Makers**
Thin supply lines crack and leak slowly behind the fridge for weeks.

**Air Conditioners**
Clogged condensate lines and rusted drain pans, especially in attic units during hot Texas summers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is appliance leak damage covered by insurance?**
Sudden failures are usually covered. The appliance itself often is not.

**Can my kitchen cabinets be saved?**
Often yes, if we get to them quickly. Particle board cabinets swell faster than solid wood.

**My water heater is in the attic and leaked. What now?**
Shut off the water and power to the heater, keep clear of the ceiling below and call us right away.

**How long does it take to dry under a dishwasher?**
Usually three to four days with the right equipment.
