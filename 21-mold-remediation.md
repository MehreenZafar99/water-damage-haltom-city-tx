## 01. Signs You May Have Mold

- A musty, earthy smell that will not go away
- Black, green or white spots on walls, ceilings or baseboards
- Stains that keep coming back after cleaning
- Peeling paint or warped drywall
- Allergy symptoms that get better when you leave the house

---

## 02. Why Mold Grows So Easily Here

Haltom City sees warm temperatures most of the year and humid spells in spring and summer. Add a hidden leak, poor attic ventilation or a clogged AC drain line, and mold has everything it needs.

## 03. Our Remediation Process

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Inspect** and find the moisture source.
2. **Contain** the area with plastic barriers and negative air pressure.
3. **Filter the air** with HEPA air scrubbers.
4. **Remove** moldy drywall, insulation and other porous materials.
5. **Clean and treat** framing and hard surfaces.
6. **Dry** the area fully.
7. **Rebuild** with new materials.

We follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation.

---

## 04. Removal vs Killing Mold

Spraying bleach on mold does not solve the problem. It can leave roots behind in porous materials and dead mold can still trigger allergies. Proper remediation physically removes the growth and fixes the moisture so it does not return.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I clean mold myself?**
Small spots on hard surfaces, under about 10 square feet, can be handled with care. Larger areas or mold inside walls should be done professionally.

**Is black mold dangerous?**
Any mold can affect health, especially for people with allergies or asthma. The color does not tell you how harmful it is.

**Will mold come back?**
Not if the moisture source is fixed and the area is dried properly.

**Does insurance cover mold?**
Coverage varies. Mold from a covered water event is more likely to be covered than mold from long term humidity.
