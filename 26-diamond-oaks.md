## 01. Why Trees Cause Water Damage in Diamond Oaks

Large oaks and pecans send roots toward any source of moisture, and older sewer lines are a favorite target. Roots slip into small cracks, grow and eventually block or break the pipe. The result is often a sewage backup through a tub, shower or floor drain.

Trees also drop branches onto roofs during storms, opening the way for rain to get into attics and ceilings.
