## 01. The Problem With Original Pipes

Homes from the mid 1900s often used galvanized steel water lines. Over time, these rust from the inside, narrow and eventually leak or split. Signs include:
- Brown or rusty water from taps
- Low water pressure
- Pinhole leaks that show up as wet spots on walls or ceilings
- Leaks at joints under sinks

A single failed joint behind a wall can soak drywall and insulation for weeks before you see it.
