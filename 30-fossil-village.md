## 01. How Drainage Problems Become Water Damage

1. Rain falls and runs toward the house because of flat or reversed grading.
2. Gutters overflow or downspouts dump water right at the foundation.
3. Water soaks the soil around the slab.
4. Moisture seeps through cracks or under exterior walls.
5. Baseboards swell, carpet gets damp and mold can start.

---

## 02. Signs to Watch For

- Damp carpet along exterior walls after rain
- Swollen or stained baseboards
- Musty smell in rooms at the edge of the house
- Puddles in the yard near the foundation that last for days
- Efflorescence (white powder) on garage slab or walls

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is my carpet wet only after it rains?**
That usually points to outside water getting in near the foundation, not a plumbing leak.

**Can you fix my yard drainage?**
We handle the water damage inside. We can explain what we see so a landscaper or drainage contractor can fix the grading.

**How long does it take to dry a slab?**
Often five to seven days, depending on how long water sat.

**Will this keep happening?**
If drainage is not fixed, yes. Correcting gutters and grading usually stops it.
