## 01. Quick Slab Leak Self Check

1. Turn off every faucet, appliance and irrigation system.
2. Look at your water meter and note the reading.
3. Wait 30 minutes without using water.
4. Check the meter again.

If the numbers moved, water is going somewhere. A slab leak is one possibility.

---

## 02. Other Warning Signs

- A warm spot on tile or wood floors
- Damp carpet with no spill nearby
- A water bill that keeps climbing
- New cracks in floors or walls
- Sound of running water with everything off

---

## 03. Drought Then Rain

Slab leaks often show up after a long dry spell followed by heavy rain. The soil shrinks, then swells quickly, and the foundation shifts. That is the moment a weak pipe is most likely to crack.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I know if it is a slab leak or something else?**
The meter test above is a good start. Our moisture inspection can confirm where water is collecting.

**Will you have to break up my floor?**
We do not break concrete. A plumber may need access to repair the pipe, and we handle the drying and restoration after.

**How long does drying take after a slab leak?**
Usually five to seven days.

**Is it urgent?**
Yes. Ongoing leaks can damage flooring, grow mold and affect the foundation over time.
