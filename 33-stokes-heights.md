## 01. How an AC Leak Happens

Your AC pulls moisture out of the air, which drips into a drain pan and runs outside through a small line. Over time that line fills with algae and sludge. When it clogs, the pan overflows. If the pan is rusted or missing a float switch, water goes straight onto the ceiling below.

---

## 02. Warning Signs

- Water stains near air vents or the attic hatch
- Dripping from ceiling vents
- A musty smell when the AC turns on
- Water pooling near the indoor unit
- AC shutting off unexpectedly (a float switch tripped)

---

## 03. Simple Prevention

- Pour a cup of white vinegar down the condensate line every few months
- Have the AC serviced each spring
- Ask your HVAC tech to add a float switch if you do not have one
- Check the drain pan for rust

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I keep running the AC after a leak?**
Turn it off until the drain line is cleared and the pan is checked. Running it adds more water.

**Do you fix AC units?**
We handle the water damage. An HVAC technician clears the line and repairs the unit.

**Is AC leak damage covered by insurance?**
Sudden overflow damage is often covered. Damage from long term neglect may not be.
