## 01. After a Hailstorm, Check These

- Granules from shingles collecting in gutters
- Dents on gutters, vents and AC units
- Missing or cracked shingles visible from the ground
- New ceiling stains after the next rain
- Damp insulation in the attic

---

## 02. Why Hail Leaks Show Up Late

Hail bruises shingles without always breaking through. Weeks later, rain seeps through those weak spots slowly. By the time a stain shows on your ceiling, insulation and decking may have been wet for a while. That delay is why an inspection after any big storm is smart.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you tarp my roof the same day?**
In most cases, yes.

**Is hail damage covered by homeowners insurance?**
Usually yes. Check your deductible, since many Texas policies have a separate wind and hail deductible.

**Do you replace roofs?**
No. We protect the roof with a tarp and restore the inside. A roofer handles the replacement.

**How do I know if my attic is wet?**
Look for damp or matted insulation and water stains on the decking. We can check for you for free.
