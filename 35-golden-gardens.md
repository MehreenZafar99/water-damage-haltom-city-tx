# Water Damage Restoration in Golden Gardens, Haltom City

Many older homes in Golden Gardens were built with cast iron drain pipes under the slab. Cast iron lasts a long time, but after 50 or more years it can rust, crack and collapse. When it fails, dirty water backs up into tubs, showers and floors. Call **(817) XXX-XXXX**.

---

## 01. Signs of a Failing Drain Line

- Slow drains in more than one fixture
- Gurgling sounds from drains or toilets
- Sewer smell inside the home
- Water coming up in the tub when you flush
- Damp flooring near bathrooms or the kitchen
- Frequent clogs that keep coming back

---

## 02. Why This Water Is Not Clean

Drain water is gray or black water. It carries soap, food waste and sewage. That means it cannot just be dried. It has to be removed, and the area cleaned and disinfected first.

---

## 03. Our Cleanup Process

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Stop using water** in the home until the line is checked.
2. **Extract** the backup water.
3. **Remove** contaminated flooring and drywall.
4. **Disinfect** all surfaces.
5. **Dry** the slab and walls.
6. **Rebuild** once the plumber replaces the line.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long do cast iron pipes last?**
Often 50 to 75 years, depending on conditions. Many Golden Gardens homes are now in that range.

**Can you replace the drain line?**
A licensed plumber handles the line. We clean up, dry and restore after.

**Is drain backup covered by insurance?**
Only with certain coverage, like a sewer backup endorsement. We can help you check your policy.

**Is it safe to stay home?**
Stay out of affected rooms and avoid using water until the problem is fixed.
