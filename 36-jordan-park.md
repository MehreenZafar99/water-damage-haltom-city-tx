## 01. Common Bathroom Leak Sources

**Toilet wax rings** that wear out and let water seep under the floor.
**Shower pans** that crack under old tile.
**Grout and caulk** that break down around tubs and showers.
**Supply lines** to toilets and sinks that split.
**Overflows** from clogged toilets or tubs.

---

## 02. Signs of a Bathroom Leak

- Soft or spongy floor near the toilet
- Loose or cracked tiles
- Stains on the ceiling below an upstairs bathroom
- Peeling paint or swollen baseboards outside the bathroom
- Musty smell after showers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is the floor soft around my toilet?**
Usually a worn wax ring has let water soak the subfloor. It needs attention before the floor weakens more.

**Can you repair the tile?**
We restore the damaged area, including flooring. For full bathroom remodels, we can point you to a contractor.

**Does insurance cover slow bathroom leaks?**
Often not, since they are gradual. Sudden overflows or burst supply lines are more likely covered.

**Is mold likely behind my shower?**
If a leak has been going on for a while, it is possible. We check during inspection.
