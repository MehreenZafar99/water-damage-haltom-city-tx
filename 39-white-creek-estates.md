## 02. Signs of a Hidden Kitchen Leak

- Swollen or peeling cabinet bottoms
- Warped flooring in front of the dishwasher
- Musty smell under the sink
- Stains on the toe kick at the base of cabinets
- Soft flooring near the fridge

---

## 03. Saving Your Cabinets

Base cabinets are often made from particle board, which swells and crumbles when wet. Acting quickly gives us the best chance to dry them in place instead of replacing them.

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Shut off** the water supply.
2. **Empty** cabinets and remove toe kicks.
3. **Extract** water from under cabinets and flooring.
4. **Dry** with targeted air under the cabinets.
5. **Check** the subfloor and walls behind.
6. **Repair** or replace damaged parts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can water damaged cabinets be saved?**
Often, if we act fast. Particle board that has swelled a lot usually needs replacing.

**Do you remove the dishwasher?**
If needed, yes, to dry the floor and wall behind it.

**Is a dishwasher leak covered by insurance?**
Sudden leaks usually are. Slow leaks over time often are not.

**How long does kitchen drying take?**
Most kitchens dry in three to four days.
