## Common Water Problems Across the City

**Older plumbing.** A large share of homes date from the 1950s through 1970s, so aging supply lines and drain pipes fail more often.

**Slab movement.** Expansive clay soil shifts with wet and dry seasons, which can crack pipes under foundations.

**Creek runoff.** Streets near Big Fossil Creek and Little Fossil Creek can take on water during flash floods.

**Storm season.** Spring hail and wind damage roofs, and water finds its way into attics and ceilings.
