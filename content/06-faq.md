## 01. Before the Crew Arrives

**What is the first thing I should do?**
Stop the water. Shut off the main valve, usually found near the street or where the line enters your home. Then call us.

**Is it safe to use electricity?**
Not in wet rooms. If you can reach the breaker without standing in water, shut off power to those areas.

**Should I start cleaning up myself?**
Moving valuables is fine. Avoid tearing out materials or using a household vacuum on water, and never touch sewage water.

## 02. Cost and Insurance

**How much does water damage restoration cost?**
It depends on how much water, what type it is and how many materials are affected. Small jobs may be a few hundred dollars while larger losses cost more. We give a clear estimate after inspection.

**Does insurance pay for it?**
Sudden events like burst pipes or appliance failures are usually covered. Gradual leaks and outside flood water often are not, unless you have separate flood coverage.

**Can you bill my insurance directly?**
In many cases, yes. We work with most major carriers.

## 03. The Drying Process

**How long does it take?**
Most jobs take three to five days of drying, plus any repairs.

**Why are the machines so loud?**
Air movers push air across wet surfaces to speed up evaporation. The noise is normal and temporary.

**Can I turn the equipment off at night?**
Please do not. Turning it off slows drying and raises mold risk.

**How do you know it is dry?**
We take moisture readings daily and compare them with dry areas of your home. We stop only when they match.

## 04. Mold and Health

**How soon can mold grow?**
Mold can start in 24 to 48 hours on wet materials.

**Will you test for mold?**
We inspect for visible growth and signs of hidden mold. If needed, we can arrange testing.

**Is gray or black water dangerous?**
Yes. Gray water from appliances and black water from sewage or floods can carry bacteria. Our crew uses protective gear and disinfects everything.
