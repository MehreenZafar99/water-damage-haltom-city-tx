## 02. What Is Usually Covered

Standard Texas homeowners policies often cover damage that is sudden and accidental, such as:
- Burst or frozen pipes
- Water heater or washing machine failures
- A supply line that breaks under a sink
- Roof leaks caused by a storm

## What Is Often Not Covered
- Slow leaks that went on for weeks or months
- Rising flood water from outside, unless you have flood insurance
- Damage from lack of maintenance

Every policy is different. We help you understand yours, but your insurer makes the final call.

---

## 03. Steps to File a Strong Claim

1. **Stop the water** and prevent more damage. Insurers expect this.
2. **Call us** so drying starts right away and damage is documented.
3. **Call your insurance company** to open a claim and get a claim number.
4. **Keep receipts** for anything you spend, like hotel stays.
5. **Do not throw out damaged items** until the adjuster has seen them or approved it.
