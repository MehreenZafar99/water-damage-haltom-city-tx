## 01. How Flooding Happens in Haltom City

- **Flash flooding** during intense spring and fall storms
- **Creek overflow** along Big Fossil Creek and Little Fossil Creek
- **Low spots** in yards and driveways that funnel water toward the foundation
- **Clogged storm drains** that back water up into streets and garages
- **Poor grading** around older homes that slopes toward the house

---

## 02. Why Flood Water Is Different

Flood water picks up soil, fertilizer, oil, sewage and bacteria as it moves. The industry classifies it as Category 3, the most contaminated type. That means:

- Soaked carpet, pad and insulation usually must be removed
- Drywall touched by flood water is cut out above the water line
- Every surface is cleaned and disinfected before drying
- Crews wear protective gear

## 04. Flood Insurance Is Separate

Standard homeowners insurance usually does not cover rising outside water. That coverage comes from a separate flood policy through NFIP or a private insurer. We document everything so you have what you need for whichever policy applies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can flooded carpet be saved?**
Usually not. Flood water is contaminated, so carpet and pad are almost always replaced.

**Is it safe to go back in my house?**
Not until power is checked and the water is removed. Avoid touching flood water without gloves and boots.

**How high do you cut the drywall?**
Typically 12 to 24 inches above the highest water line, depending on how far moisture wicked up.

**Do you pump out garages too?**
Yes. We handle garages, living spaces and crawl spaces.
