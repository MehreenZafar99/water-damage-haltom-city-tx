## 01. Common Causes of Roof Leaks Here

- **Hail damage.** North Texas gets some of the most frequent hail in the country, and it cracks shingles and dents flashing.
- **Wind damage.** Strong storm winds lift and tear shingles.
- **Worn roofs.** Many older Haltom City homes are on their second or third roof, and age shows.
- **Flashing failures** around chimneys, vents and skylights.
- **Clogged gutters** that back water under the roof edge.

---

## 02. Where Roof Water Ends Up

**Attic insulation.** Soaks up water like a sponge and loses its value.
**Roof decking and rafters.** Wet wood can rot and grow mold.
**Ceiling drywall.** Stains, bubbles, sags or collapses.
**Walls.** Water runs down inside wall cavities.
**Floors.** Drips land on carpet and hardwood below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you fix roofs?**
We handle the water damage inside. We can help with a temporary tarp and point you to a roofer for the permanent fix.

**Will insurance pay for roof leak damage?**
Storm related leaks are often covered. Leaks from an old, worn roof may not be.

**Can wet insulation be dried?**
Some types can, but blown in insulation that is soaked is usually removed and replaced.

**My ceiling is sagging. What should I do?**
Keep everyone out from under it and call us now. A sagging ceiling can hold a lot of water and may fall.
