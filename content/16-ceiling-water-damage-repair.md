## 01. What Your Ceiling Is Telling You

| What You See | What It Usually Means |
|---|---|
| Yellow or brown stain | A past or slow leak from the roof, AC or plumbing |
| Bubbling paint | Water trapped between paint and drywall |
| Sagging or bulging | Water pooling above, may fall soon |
| Dripping | Active leak, call right away |
| Dark spots | Possible mold growth |

---

## 02. Where Ceiling Water Comes From

- **Roof leaks** from storm or hail damage
- **AC condensate lines** in the attic that clog and overflow, very common in Texas summers
- **Upstairs bathrooms** with leaking toilets, tubs or showers
- **Plumbing lines** running through the attic
- **Water heaters** installed in the attic

---

## 03. How We Repair Ceiling Water Damage

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Find the source** with moisture meters and thermal imaging.
2. **Relieve trapped water** safely from sagging areas.
3. **Remove wet insulation** and damaged drywall.
4. **Dry the framing** above the ceiling.
5. **Replace drywall** and match your ceiling texture, like popcorn or knockdown.
6. **Prime and paint** with stain blocking primer.

---

## 04. Why Paint Alone Will Not Fix It

Painting over a stain hides it but does not solve the moisture behind it. If the area is still wet, the stain comes back and mold can grow out of sight. Always dry and check it first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I just paint over the stain?**
Only after the source is fixed and the area is confirmed dry, and only with a stain blocking primer.

**Can you match popcorn or textured ceilings?**
Yes. We match common ceiling textures so the repair blends in.

**Is a sagging ceiling dangerous?**
Yes. It can collapse under the weight of the water. Stay clear and call us.

**Does my AC cause ceiling leaks?**
It can. Clogged condensate lines and full drain pans in the attic are a common cause during summer.
