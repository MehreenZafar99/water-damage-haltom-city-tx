## 01. Why Slab Leaks Are Common Here

**Clay soil.** North Texas clay swells when wet and shrinks during drought. That movement stresses pipes under the foundation.
**Older copper lines.** Decades of use wear pipes thin where they rub against concrete.
**Hard water.** Minerals in the water slowly eat away at pipes.
**Poor installation.** Pipes bent or kinked during original construction can fail years later.

---

## 02. Signs of a Slab Leak

- Warm or hot spots on the floor (hot water line leak)
- Water bill higher than normal with no change in use
- Sound of running water when everything is off
- Damp carpet or warped flooring with no clear source
- Cracks in walls or flooring
- Mold or musty smell near the floor
- Low water pressure

## 03. What We Do After a Slab Leak

Once a plumber finds and repairs the leak, we handle the water damage it left behind.

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Map the moisture** across floors and lower walls.
2. **Remove damaged flooring** that trapped water, like carpet pad or laminate.
3. **Dry the slab** with dehumidifiers and specialty mats.
4. **Dry lower walls** and baseboards.
5. **Treat for mold** where needed.
6. **Reinstall flooring** and trim.

If you have not found the leak yet, our moisture readings can help narrow down where it is.

---

## 04. Why Concrete Takes Longer to Dry

Concrete holds a lot of water and releases it slowly. Putting new flooring on a wet slab traps moisture and can ruin the floor. We test the slab before any new flooring goes down.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does insurance cover slab leaks?**
Many policies cover the water damage and sometimes the cost of accessing the pipe. The pipe repair itself is often excluded.

**Do you find and fix the leak?**
We help locate wet areas. A licensed plumber repairs the pipe, and we restore everything after.

**How long does a slab take to dry?**
Often five to seven days, depending on how long it leaked.

**Can a slab leak damage my foundation?**
Yes. Long term leaks can wash out soil and affect the foundation, so act quickly.
