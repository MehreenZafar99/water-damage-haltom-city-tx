## 01. Storm Season in Haltom City

Spring, roughly March through June, brings the most severe weather to Tarrant County. That includes:
- **Hail** from golf ball size up, which damages roofs, skylights and siding
- **Straight line winds** that knock down trees and lift shingles
- **Heavy downpours** that cause flash flooding
- **Winter storms** with ice and hard freezes that burst pipes

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does homeowners insurance cover storm damage?**
Wind and hail damage are usually covered. Rising flood water requires separate flood insurance.

**Can you tarp my roof tonight?**
In most cases, yes. We offer emergency tarping to stop more water from getting in.

**Should I wait for my adjuster before cleanup?**
No. Protect your property and start drying right away. We document everything for the adjuster.

**Do you fix roofs after storms?**
We handle tarping and interior water damage. A roofer handles the permanent roof repair.
