## 02. Signs of Water Damage in Floors

- **Carpet:** soggy feel, musty smell, dark spots, loose seams
- **Hardwood:** cupping (edges higher than center), crowning, dark stains, gaps
- **Laminate:** swelling at edges, peaking, soft spots
- **Vinyl:** lifting, bubbling, water between planks
- **Tile:** loose tiles, cracked grout, hollow sounds

## 03. Our Flooring Restoration Process

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Assess** the flooring type and water category.
2. **Extract** water from the surface and underneath.
3. **Lift or remove** what cannot be saved, like carpet pad or swollen laminate.
4. **Dry** the floor and subfloor with mats, dehumidifiers and air movers.
5. **Clean and sanitize** carpet if it can be saved.
6. **Reinstall or replace** flooring.

---

## 04. Why the Subfloor Matters

Even if the top layer looks fine, water under it can rot wood subfloors or keep a concrete slab wet for weeks. New flooring on a wet subfloor fails quickly. We always check below the surface.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can wet carpet be saved?**
Often, if the water was clean and we dry it fast. Carpet touched by sewage or flood water should be replaced.

**My hardwood is cupping. Is it ruined?**
Not always. Mild cupping can flatten out with proper drying. Severe damage may need sanding or replacement.

**Why does laminate need replacing?**
Laminate has a pressed wood core that swells when wet and does not return to its original shape.

**Can you match my existing flooring?**
We will try to match it. If the product is discontinued, we help you find the closest option.
