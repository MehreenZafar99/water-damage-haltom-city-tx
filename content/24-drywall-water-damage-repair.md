# Drywall Water Damage Repair in Haltom City

Drywall acts like a sponge. Once water reaches it, it wicks upward, swells and loses strength. We decide what can be dried, what needs to go and then repair it so the wall looks like nothing happened.

## 01. Dry It or Replace It?

**Drywall can often be dried in place when:**
- The water was clean
- We get there within a day or two
- The drywall is still firm and not crumbling

**Drywall usually needs replacing when:**
- It is soft, swollen or sagging
- The water was gray or black (appliance drain, sewage, flood)
- Mold has started growing
- Insulation behind it is soaked

---

## 02. What a Flood Cut Is

When the bottom of a wall is damaged, we cut a straight line across the drywall, usually 2 to 4 feet up. This removes the wet section, lets air reach inside the wall and gives a clean line for the repair. It is faster and cheaper than replacing entire walls.

---

## 03. Our Drywall Repair Process

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Measure moisture** in the wall and how high it traveled.
2. **Remove baseboards** and cut damaged drywall.
3. **Remove wet insulation** from the wall cavity.
4. **Dry the framing** fully.
5. **Hang new drywall**, tape and mud.
6. **Match texture**, like orange peel or knockdown.
7. **Prime, paint and reinstall** baseboards.

---

## 04. Signs of Water Damaged Drywall

- Bubbling or peeling paint
- Brown or yellow stains
- Soft or spongy spots when pressed
- Swelling along the bottom of walls
- Musty smell
- Visible mold at baseboards

**How high will you cut my drywall?**
Usually 12 to 24 inches above the highest wet point, based on moisture readings.

**How long does drywall repair take?**
After drying, repairs typically take two to four days including texture and paint.
