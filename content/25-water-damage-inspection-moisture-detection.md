## 01. When to Get an Inspection

- A musty smell with no clear cause
- A water bill that jumped for no reason
- Stains or bubbling paint
- After a leak was fixed, to make sure everything dried
- Before buying or selling a home in Haltom City
- After a storm, to check the attic and ceilings
- Floors that feel soft, warm or damp

---

## 02. Tools We Use

**Thermal Imaging Cameras**
Wet areas show up cooler than dry ones, so we can see water patterns behind walls without cutting.

**Pinless Moisture Meters**
Scan surfaces like drywall and flooring without damage.

**Pin Moisture Meters**
Give exact readings in wood, drywall and other materials.

**Hygrometers**
Measure humidity in rooms, attics and crawl spaces.

## 04. Why Early Detection Saves Money

A small hidden leak found early might need a day of drying. The same leak found months later can mean new drywall, flooring and mold removal. An inspection now can prevent a much bigger bill later.
