## 01. Why Converted Garages Flood Easily

- **Water heaters** left in or next to the converted room
- **Laundry hookups** with old rubber hoses
- **Lower floor level** than the rest of the home, so water collects there
- **Slab floors** that stay wet longer under carpet or laminate
- **Old garage doors** replaced with walls that may let in rain at the base

---

## 02. Signs Your Water Heater Is Close to Failing

- Rust colored water from hot taps
- Rumbling or popping noises
- Moisture or small puddles around the base
- A tank more than 10 years old

A tank failure can release 40 to 50 gallons in minutes, and it keeps filling until the water is shut off.

---

## 03. How We Restore Converted Spaces

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Shut off** water to the heater or washer.
2. **Extract** water from carpet or flooring.
3. **Check the slab** and lower walls for moisture.
4. **Remove** swollen baseboards or wet pad.
5. **Dry** the room fully.
6. **Replace** flooring and trim.

Yes, though the pad is often replaced. We make sure the slab is dry before new flooring goes down.
