## 01. Runoff vs Plumbing Water

Water from a pipe is usually clean. Runoff and flood water is not. It carries soil, lawn chemicals and bacteria, which means:
- Carpet and pad usually need to be removed
- Walls touched by the water need cleaning or cutting
- Everything must be disinfected before drying

Knowing where the water came from changes how we clean it up.

---

## 02. Where Water Gets In

- Under exterior doors and garage doors
- Through foundation cracks
- Around window wells and low windows
- Backing up through floor drains when storm systems overflow

---

## 04. Reduce Your Risk

- Keep gutters and downspouts clear, with extensions away from the house
- Make sure soil slopes away from the foundation
- Keep storm drains near your home free of debris
- Look into flood insurance, since most homeowners policies exclude outside flooding
