## 01. Freeze Risk in Older Homes

North Texas does not freeze often, but when it does, it can be severe, as the February 2021 winter storm showed. Older homes are more exposed because of:
- Thin or missing insulation around pipes
- Water lines running through unheated attics
- Hose bibs without freeze protection
- Plumbing in detached garages, sheds and workshops

---

## 02. Freeze Prep Checklist

- Wrap exposed pipes in attics and crawl spaces
- Let faucets drip on the coldest nights
- Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls
- Disconnect garden hoses
- Know where your main shutoff valve is
- Keep heat on if you travel in winter
