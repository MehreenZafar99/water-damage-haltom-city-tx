Garden of Eden sits close to Little Fossil Creek, with lots of trees and green space. Low ground and shade can keep soil and walls damp after rainy weeks. Over time, that moisture invites mold. Call **(817) XXX-XXXX** if you notice a musty smell that will not go away.

---

## 01. Why Moisture Lingers Here

- Shade from mature trees slows drying around the house
- Low lying ground holds water after rain
- Older homes may have less insulation and ventilation
- Crawl spaces under older homes can trap humid air

---

## 02. Signs of a Moisture Problem

- Musty smell, especially in closets and corners
- Dark spots on walls or ceilings
- Condensation on windows
- Warped wood trim or doors that stick
- Allergy symptoms that ease when you leave the house

---

## 03. Our Process

**Timeline Strip**

1. **Inspect** and measure humidity and moisture.
2. **Find the source**, whether ground water, leaks or poor airflow.
3. **Contain** any mold growth.
4. **Remove** affected materials.
5. **Dry** the space and lower humidity.
6. **Recommend** ventilation or drainage changes.
