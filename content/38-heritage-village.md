## 01. Common Leaks in New Homes

- **Loose plumbing fittings** that slowly drip inside walls
- **Nail or screw punctures** in pipes from drywall or trim work
- **Improperly sealed showers** and window flashing
- **Appliance hookups** like ice maker and washer lines not tightened
- **PEX connections** that were crimped incorrectly

---

## 02. Builder Warranty vs Insurance

Many new homes come with a builder warranty that covers plumbing defects for a period of time. Insurance may cover the resulting water damage. Knowing which one applies matters, and good documentation helps with both.

We provide:
- Photos of the damage and the source
- Moisture readings from day one
- A written report you can share with your builder and insurer

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I call my builder or you first?**
Stop the water, then call us to begin drying and documenting. Then notify your builder. Waiting can make damage worse.

**Will fixing the damage void my warranty?**
Emergency drying to prevent more damage is expected. We document everything so your builder can see what happened.

**Can you match my new flooring and paint?**
Yes. We match finishes as closely as possible.

**Why would a new home leak?**
Small installation errors, like a loose fitting, are common and often show up in the first year or two.
